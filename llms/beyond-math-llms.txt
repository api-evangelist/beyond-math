# Beyond Math

> BeyondMath is an AI company that has built a foundational AI model of physics — a Generative Physics Engine that runs full-fidelity, engineering-grade simulations (fluid, thermal, and structural) directly from uploaded geometry, without pre-meshing or preprocessing, delivering results up to 1000x faster than traditional CFD/FEA solvers. The platform serves automotive, aerospace, energy, defense, semiconductor, construction, telecommunications, and electronics engineering teams. Access is currently gated behind a waitlist for the Generative Physics Studio; no public developer API, SDK, or OpenAPI is published at this time.
